V8: a one-third scale Chevrolet small-block 350. Printed block and heads, built-up cross-plane crank with flywheel and pulley, eight rods and pistons, firing order 1-8-4-3-6-5-7-2. The crank turns a 2:1 timing gear, the camshaft, and through sixteen lobes the straps, lifters, pushrods, rockers and valves. One gearmotor and four AA cells run it.

Build it in Tangware Studio, which runs the checks.

The valvetrain is desmodromic: each lobe is a round eccentric held in a strap, and each valve hangs from a toggle link that near-dwells it shut, because the library has no cam follower. So there are no valve springs, and a valve eases open rather than snapping, wider than a real cam's lobe. Pistons and valvetrain carry a hand check instead of finite elements, since stress does not resolve sliding joints.

Dimensions: bore, stroke, rod and deck height of the 350 as published by Speedway Motors and JEGS, scaled; valve lift, gears and rail sizes are estimates. Motor, battery holder and switch: Pololu 3069, MPD BC4AAW and E-Switch RD11131121; their maker CAD is downloaded on the first build. The rocker switch sits in a wall at the back of the battery tray. The cells are Energizer E91 AA, modelled at their datasheet's largest size, since no maker CAD exists for them.
