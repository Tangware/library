# pantilt requirements

R-numbered outcomes, intent only. `▸` marks the person's words, `[derived]` agent synthesis. Founder approval: not given (interview 0018).

## R1 It holds any phone 60–85 mm wide
▸ "fits any phone 60–85 mm wide"

## R2 It turns far enough to follow a teacher across the front of the desk
[derived] A quarter turn each way from facing the teacher: beyond it the teacher is beside the phone, out of any lesson. From ▸ "turns and tilts to keep my face in frame".

## R3 It tilts far enough to follow a teacher from sitting to standing
[derived] Up to a third of a right angle up, a sixth down: standing up at arm's length raises the face that far. From ▸ "turns and tilts".

## R4 Its motion is smooth and silent
▸ "smooth, silent motion"
[derived] No gear train anywhere in the drive: each axis turns on its motor's own shaft, so there is no gear noise and no backlash.

## R5 It holds the heaviest phone at any pose without slipping or tipping
[derived] From ▸ "fits any phone"; a stand that drops or tips the phone on a call has failed.

## R6 It finds the face itself, on the device, during any video call, with no account
▸ "cloud accounts" (refused). [derived] The sensor tilts with the phone so it looks where the phone looks.

## R7 It runs from a phone charger, plugged in where nothing moves
[derived] A lesson runs for hours; a plug on a moving part would pull on the motion.

## R8 It lands under its cost target
▸ "500 units. Target landed cost under $100; it sells at about $99."

## Shape rules
## S1 It shows one lens and nothing that reads as a face
▸ "a face or eyes on the device" (refused). [derived] One window, centred under the phone.

## S2 It reads as a camera tool: its two axes show as plain cylinders at right angles
▸ "it reads as a camera tool, not a toy robot."

## S3 One matte material and colour on every made part
▸ "anything that looks cheap in the background of a video" (refused). [derived] Gloss and mixed plastics are the cheap codes in `discover/category.md`.

## S4 The base is no wider than the widest phone it holds
[derived] The leading stand's base was called bulky (T3, `discover/evidence.md`); a base wider than the phone reads as an appliance, not a tool.
