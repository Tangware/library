## Failed-products set
- **Kuri** (Mayfield Robotics, 2018): ceased production before shipping; failure mode per reports: no business fit at its parent and a $700 price for limited function. https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/25/mayfield-robotics-ceases-production-of-kuri-robot-amid-a-questionable-future (search summary). Lesson [derived]: a face and personality raise price and expectations past what a camera job pays for.
- **Jibo** (2018): delayed shipments, IP sold off; https://gizmodo.com/consumer-robots-had-a-terrible-horrible-no-good-very-1830832094 (search summary). Lesson [derived]: a robot with a face is a companion, judged as one; ours must stay a tool.
- **Unnamed cheap tracking stand** (Kent Faith review, Jan 2021): motor "zzzzzzt" audible in recordings; https://www.kentfaith.com/review_97699 (search summary). Lesson: noise kills the product for anyone on a call.
