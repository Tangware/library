# Audiences

▸ "Buyer = user; influencer = their students, who see the video."

## Buyer and user: the online teacher or streamer
▸ "online teachers and streamers."
At a glance [derived]: it will hold my phone, it will not fall, it is worth $99 next to my other kit, it does not need an account.

## Influencer: the students
At a glance [derived]: they never see the stand, only the frame it makes; what they read is whether the camera moves like a camera operator (calm, level) or like a machine (jerks, hunts, buzzes).

## Conflicts
### C1 The teacher wants to forget the stand; the students read its motion
Shape serves: the teacher [derived] (founder: not answered). The shape is a quiet camera tool on the desk.
Students served through: motion character — slow starts and stops, no hunting, no sound on the call [derived], from ▸ "smooth, silent motion".

### C2 A teacher hides it behind the phone; a streamer shows it on camera
Shape serves: seen on camera [derived] (founder: not answered), from ▸ "anything that looks cheap in the background of a video."
Teacher served through: a small footprint and a matte finish that disappears on a desk [derived].

### C3 The buyer pays for "it follows me"; the refusal forbids anything that looks at you
Shape serves: the refusal. One lens, read as a camera's, never two and never placed as eyes [derived], from ▸ "a face or eyes on the device".
Buyer served through: packaging and voice say "follows you"; the object does not perform it at rest.

## Character
Start: archetypes Caregiver and Everyman, resolved to one named character [derived] (founder: not answered): **the camera operator** — present for the whole shoot, silent, moves only to keep the shot, never in it.

Rejected archetypes and what each forbids:
- Jester: forbids bright colour, a cute pose, bounce or wiggle at idle.
- Explorer/Hero: forbids rugged or "action" codes (rubber armour, chunky bolts).
- Innocent/Lover: forbids a face, eyes, a soft pet-like body.
- Magician: forbids light shows or a glowing "AI" ring.
- Ruler: forbids gloss and chrome that make the stand the star on camera.
