# Formal

| Dichotomy | Position | Attribute | Forbids |
|---|---|---|---|
| order / complexity | order | precise | more than two cylinder axes; decoration |
| reduction / richness | reduction | crew | ribs, grilles, texture on the outside |
| primitive / sculpted | primitive | honest | freeform surfaces, lofts |
| continuous / articulated | articulated | honest | one skin over both motions |
| concealed / exposed fixings | concealed from the front, exposed underneath | crew | screw heads on any face the camera or teacher sees |
| uniform / varied materials | uniform | crew (S3) | a second colour on made parts |
| sharp / soft | sharp, edges broken small | precise | large soft radii that read as a toy |
| light / heavy | heavy at the bottom | solid | a base lighter-looking than the head |
| symmetric / asymmetric | asymmetric (one-sided arm) | honest | a fork that doubles the parts to look symmetric |
| matte / gloss | matte | crew (S3) | any gloss |

## Hierarchy of emphasis
- Dominant: the phone (it is the camera).
- Subdominant: the tilt head, a horizontal cylinder.
- Subordinate: the base cylinder and the arm.

## Surface continuity
- Base, turret, cradle: C⁰ with small broken edges; no C² blends.

Shape rules carried to `prd.md`: S1 (P1), S2 (P3), S3 (P4), S4 (P6).
