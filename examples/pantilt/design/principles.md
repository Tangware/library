# Principles

Root: **crew, not cast** (`discover/story.md`).

## P1 Nothing looks back at you
Trace: ▸ "a face or eyes on the device".
Forbids: two openings side by side on any face; a lens set high on a rounded head; any light or mark beside the lens; a visor or brow.

## P2 It moves like a camera operator, never like a creature
Trace: ▸ "smooth, silent motion"; ▸ "not a toy robot".
Forbids: idle drift, "breathing", nods, wiggles, greeting moves; any motion that is not keeping the shot; a sudden start or stop.

## P3 Its axes are honest
Trace: ▸ "it reads as a camera tool"; category codes of tripod heads and gimbals.
Forbids: a ball joint or skirt that hides how it moves; a shell that pretends to be one piece over two motions.

## P4 Nothing on it reads cheap on camera
Trace: ▸ "anything that looks cheap in the background of a video".
Forbids: gloss, chrome, two plastics of different colour, visible screw heads on the front, printed text or logos on the front, a lens dome.

## P5 It asks nothing of you
Trace: ▸ "cloud accounts".
Forbids: an account, a pairing step to start, a battery to charge, a switch to remember.

## P6 It is smaller than what it holds
Trace: S4; T3's "bulky base".
Forbids: a base wider than the widest phone; a stalk taller than needed to clear the desk.

## Attributes
| Attribute | Demands | Forbids | Surfaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| quiet | direct drive, slow ramps | gears, belts, fans, clicks | drive, motion |
| precise | one governing radius, coaxial cylinders, even gaps | blends piled on shapes, uneven gaps | shell, joints |
| crew | dark matte, nothing to look at | lights, faces, logos on the front | shell, lens window |
| solid | heavy low base, phone held without wobble | thin stalks, spindly arms | base, arm |
| honest | each axis a visible cylinder | hidden joints, false seams | joints |
