# Decoy

Written by a fresh agent given only `README.md` and the core references (verbatim):

> **Lectern.** A low, matte-grey wedge about the size of a hardcover book. The phone sits upright in a soft felt-lined slot cut into the wedge's sloped top face. A single hidden magnet ring holds the phone, so there is no clamp. The wedge's top plate is the moving part. It swivels (pan) and rocks forward and back (tilt) on a sealed ball-joint under a smooth, continuous skirt. Motion shows only as the whole plate gently leaning toward you, like a head tracking you. No axes or cylinders are visible. A tiny dark sensor window sits dead-centre in the plate's front edge, just below the phone, to track the face. Materials: powder-coated aluminium with a felt insert, in warm grey. Idle: the plate slowly settles flat and level with a faint "breathing" drift, so it looks calm and alive.
