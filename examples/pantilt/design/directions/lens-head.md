# Lens head

- **Form vocabulary:** three primitives. A low cylinder base; a short one-sided arm rising from it; a horizontal tilt drum carrying the phone cradle. The pan axis is the base's axis; the tilt axis is the drum's. From X1 (radar pedestal), X2 (theodolite), D1 (gimbal).
- **Palette:** matte black, one material.
- **Motion character:** slow ramped starts and stops, no idle motion; parks facing forward, level.
- **Story:** a small camera operator's head on the desk.
- **Verdict:** chosen [derived] (founder not reached, interview 0022).
