# Tower

- **Form vocabulary:** one tall continuous cylinder; the top third turns (pan) and the phone hangs off a tilt pivot set into it. From X5 (lighthouse), D5 (ring light).
- **Palette:** matte black.
- **Motion character:** the top of the tower turns; the tilt pivot is a small disc.
- **Story:** a lighthouse that keeps its beam on you.
- **Verdict:** rejected [derived]: continuous skin over the pan seam breaks P3 weakly; a tall tower's height breaks P6 ("a stalk taller than needed") and puts the phone above eye level, so tilt is always down; the lighthouse story invites a light (P1, Magician forbidden).
