# Audiences

## Buyer
▸ "buyer = the adult child"
At a glance [derived]: that a missed dose will reach them, and that their parent will accept it on the kitchen counter.

## User
▸ "user = the elderly parent"
At a glance [derived]: which part opens (one), that nothing is small (▸ refuses "small buttons"), and that it is theirs, not a hospital's (▸ refuses "anything that looks medical or institutional").

## Influencer
▸ "influencer = the parent's doctor or pharmacist"
At a glance [derived]: that the dose cannot be taken out of order and that it holds a week of four daily doses.

## Conflicts
### C1 Influencer reads safety (locked, controlled, clinical), user reads home (not medical)
Shape serves: user ▸ refuses "anything that looks medical or institutional"
Influencer served through: the dose mechanism itself (only one dose reachable), the packaging insert and the child's app record [derived]
### C2 Buyer reads reassurance (a device that reports), user reads privacy (nothing watching)
Shape serves: user [derived] from the root refusal "never make the parent feel managed"; no camera, no face, no visible electronics
Buyer served through: the app's messages and the packaging [derived]
### C3 Influencer reads a locked refill, user reads a refill they can do
Shape serves: user ▸ "a refill that a 75-year-old can do"
Influencer served through: the carousel shows doses in day order under a lid that lifts off; tamper-proofing is not offered [derived], recorded as an open founder question

## Character
[derived] **The breadbin** — a calm kitchen object that keeps something for you and hands it over at the right time.
Rejected archetypes and what each forbids:
- Caregiver: forbids nursing cues (white-and-blue, red crosses, pill icons).
- Ruler: forbids locks and keys shown on the outside.
- Sage: forbids a display of information (screens, numbers, schedules on the face).
- Magician: forbids touch surfaces and hidden controls.
- Jester: forbids a face, eyes or a voice character.
- Hero: forbids tech cues (glowing rings, chrome).
- Explorer, Rebel: forbid rugged or aggressive form.
- Lover: forbids glossy sensual curves.
- Innocent: forbids toy pastels.
- Everyman: the start; kept as a household object, without the white-appliance residual.
