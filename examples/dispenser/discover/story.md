## Purpose and refusals
Refusals: looks medical or institutional; small buttons; a touchscreen; [derived] a voice or a face (see category.md, Pillo).
Root [derived]: **it must never make the parent feel managed.** Medical looks say "patient", small buttons and touchscreens say "you can't work this", a face says "you are being watched". New cases are decided by the root: anything the parent must learn, or that marks them as a patient, is refused.

## Story structure
[derived] The product guides the parent (it calls, it opens the dose). So the brand cannot also be the guide: the brand is the **helper to the child**, the object is the guide to the parent.
