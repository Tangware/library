## The product
A low matte drum, 238 mm across and 67 mm tall. A carousel of 28 dose pockets and one closed home sector turns under a lift-off lid; at dose time the next pocket drops through the one gate into a front drawer cut from the drum. A buzzer and one light call; a reed sensor sees the drawer open and the Pico 2 W tells the child over Wi-Fi.

## Unverified (from the manifest and the tests' does_not_catch)
- Loads and stress (M22): no load cases run; the 2 mm walls are the fit table's structural wall, not load-checked.
- Tolerance stacks and fits beyond the mesh tolerance; print allowances are the library's example fits, not calibrated.
- Printability against a partner's process table; the base's floor and the carousel's web print over supports.
- Cable routes: the leads are straight runs between solder points and are not checked against the parts they cross; the gate's bend check passes only because nothing bends.
- Every pose but rest in the gate; R1 and R5 tests check the turned carousel and the open drawer by hand.
- The buzzer's 85 dB is the maker's figure in free air; sound through the plate's bore and the 2 mm foot gap is not measured.
- Dose weight and tablet friction behind R9 are estimates; the margin to the stepper's pull-in torque is small.
- The reed sensor and actuator are datasheet models (maker STEP behind a login); the Pico 2 W is the maker's Pico 2 STEP (no W STEP is published).
- The buzzer needs a transistor (30 mA > a GPIO pin); it is billed inside its lead, not modelled.
