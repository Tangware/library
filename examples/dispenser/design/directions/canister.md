# Direction: Canister

Form vocabulary: a tall narrow cylinder (tiffin / lighthouse), doses stacked in trays; a light ring at the top; the due dose is pushed out of a slot near the top.
Palette: matte two-tone, light top band.
Motion: trays drop one level per dose.
Story: the lighthouse that lights when it's time.
Verdict: **rejected** — tall and narrow breaks "heavy, low and wide" (formal.md, Trustworthy) and a top slot is reached at shoulder height on a counter; a light ring is a smart-speaker cue (P4, Identify).

## Against the profile
Breaks: Identify (smart-speaker family), Express (fragile, tippy). Meets: Describe (one opening).
