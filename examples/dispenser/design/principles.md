# Principles

Each traces to discover/; the root is story.md's "it must never make the parent feel managed".

## P1 A kitchen thing, not a clinical one
From ▸ refuses "anything that looks medical or institutional"; audiences C1.
Forbids: a white or off-white body (the category residual, matte or not); white-and-blue or white-and-grey palettes; red crosses, pill icons or the word "medication" on the object; clear lids showing rows of pills; gloss white plastic.

## P2 One way in
From ▸ "impossible to take the wrong dose"; R1.
Forbids: more than one opening that reaches pills in use; a lid that can be opened without lifting the whole top; compartments visible from outside.

## P3 Nothing to learn
From ▸ refuses "small buttons, a touchscreen"; the root refusal.
Forbids: buttons; screens; touch areas; menus; any control that needs reading.

## P4 It speaks only when a dose is due
From ▸ "a loud, clear alert"; audiences C2.
Forbids: idle lights, status LEDs, clocks; sound outside the alert; a voice or a face.

## P5 Big moves only
From ▸ "a refill that a 75-year-old can do".
Forbids: latches, catches and tabs operated by a fingernail; parts that must be aligned by eye to go back; screws a user must touch.

## P6 Electronics are invisible
From audiences C2 (privacy) and the failed set (Pillo).
Forbids: a camera; visible boards, cables or vents; a cable leaving anywhere but low at the back.

## Attributes
| Attribute | Demands | Forbids | Surfaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calm | still at rest, one seam | idle lights, motion when no dose is due | shell, light |
| Domestic | matte, warm colour, soft edges | gloss, clinical white, chrome | shell, lid, drawer |
| Obvious | one drawer at the front, one light above it | hidden or second controls | drawer, light |
| Generous | large parts for large hands | small pulls, small gaps | drawer pull, lid edge |
| Trustworthy | one dose at a time | doses visible or reachable together | carousel, lid |
