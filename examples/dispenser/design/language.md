# Design language: Breadbin

Gate note: the direction was chosen by the agent [derived]; no full-scale model, naive-viewer reading or physical colour sample was possible (all [person]). These gate items stay open.

## Decision study
| Decision | Rule | Numeric side (source) | Counter-example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall form | one drum: base and lid share one outside diameter (S2) | the diameter is the carousel's plus walls (master sketch; carousel from R2's 28 doses) | a lid wider than the base, reading as a hat |
| Mass and footprint | low and wide: height under the diameter | formula in the master; checked by S2 test | a tall canister (rejected Canister) |
| Seams | one seam, lid on base, nothing else horizontal | — | a decorative band or groove |
| How it opens | the lid lifts straight up, whole; no hinge, no latch (P5) | lid locates on a lip, the fit table's sliding fit | a hinged lid with a catch |
| Control | the drawer only, its front cut from the drum and following its curve; pull recess wider than a hand (R7) | 5th-percentile female hand breadth, 69 mm (Pheasant, *Bodyspace*; not opened) | a flat drawer face proud of the drum; a knob |
| Special surface: light | one light, on the symmetry plane above the drawer (S3, P4) | one 5 mm LED behind a hole | a light ring; status LEDs |
| Special surface: sound | heard, not seen: no grille on the faces (S3); sound leaves under the base | sounder rated ≥ 85 dB at 10 cm (R4, datasheet) | a speaker grille on the front |
| Joints and fixings | every screw from below (S1, M29) | one M3 type for the shell | screws on the lid or the front |
| Cables | one power cable leaves low at the back (P6) | — | a cable at the side or the front |
| Edges | one governing edge radius on every outside edge | the structural wall (fit table) | sharp edges, or several radii |
| Material | one matte PETG for every printed part (S4) | — | wood knob, steel body (the decoy) |

## Signature elements
1. **The cut drawer**: the drawer is a slice of the drum. Example: the drawer front is the drum's own surface. Counter-example: a drawer added onto the drum.
2. **The one seam**: lid meets base on a single line. Counter-example: a visible gap or a second band.
3. **The single light**: one point of light on the centre line, only at dose time. Counter-example: a ring or a row.

## Colour, material, finish
Matte PETG, one colour. Residual: white and grey (category, P1). Dominant: pastel plastic (organisers). Emergent: warm earthy matte, and charcoal kitchenware (cast iron, enamel). Chosen [derived]: a warm earthy matte, Prusament PETG Matte Noctua Brown (catalogue). No physical sample was judged: open.

## Stress test
Applied to the packaging: a round carton, one seam at the lid, a pull-tab cut from the side (the cut drawer), one printed dot of colour on the centre line (the single light), no medical icons. The rules carry over without change; the language is not only a style of this object.
