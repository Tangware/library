# Decoy

Written by a fresh agent given only README.md and the three core references, asked to aim at the brief's weakest point.

> "Crock": a squat, round-shouldered drum the size of a breadbin, matte warm-white powder-coated steel, a domed lid that lifts off to fill 28 colour-keyed wedges on a carousel. At dose time the wedge drops into a big OXO-style dish behind a wide front opening; Dad lifts the dish. A Braun-style amber light ring around the opening pulses; a low warm chime "quieter than a kettle" repeats every 5 minutes. One oversized dimpled "I've taken them" button. Beech knob and dish rim, charcoal rubber base, hidden rear cord, 48 h battery.

## Kill table (first run)
| Rule | Verdict | Why |

| P3 Nothing to learn / R7 | **kills** | an "I've taken them" button, however large, is a button and a second control |
| R4 loud and clear | **kills** | a chime "quieter than a kettle" is chosen for politeness, not for a 75-year-old's hearing |
| formal: uniform materials / S4 | **kills** | steel body, beech knob and rim, rubber base: three materials on the shell |
| P1 kitchen, not clinical | survived | warm-white matte steel passed: P1 forbade only white with blue/grey and gloss white |
| P2 one way in | survived | one front opening, one dish |
| P4 speaks only when due | survived | the ring lights only at dose time |

## Sharpened and re-run
P1 was the weak point: a white body is the category residual even when matte and warm. P1 now forbids any white or off-white body. Re-run: P1 **kills** (warm-white steel). The decoy dies on P1, P3/R7, R4 and S4.
