# Formal positions

| Dichotomy | Position | Justified by | Forbids |
|---|---|---|---|
| order / complexity | order | Calm | more than two elements on any face |
| reduction / richness | reduction | Obvious | decoration, grooves, texture fields |
| primitive / sculpted | primitive (a drum) | Domestic (breadbin, canister) | organic or faceted shells |
| continuous / articulated | continuous | Calm | visible splits between base and lid other than the one seam |
| concealed / exposed fixings | concealed | P6, Domestic | screws seen from above or the front (S1) |
| uniform / varied materials | uniform | Calm | two plastics or colours on the shell (S4) |
| sharp / soft | soft | Domestic, Generous | unbroken edges on any touched part |
| light / heavy | heavy (low and wide) | Trustworthy | tall, tippy proportions |
| symmetric / asymmetric | symmetric about the front-back plane | Obvious | off-centre drawer or light |
| matte / gloss | matte | Domestic, P1 | gloss surfaces |

Hierarchy of emphasis: dominant — the drum; subdominant — the drawer front; subordinate — the light.
Surface continuity: shell C⁰ at the one seam, C¹ (rounded) at every outside edge; drawer front follows the drum's surface (C⁰ seam); lid top C¹ into its side.
