# dispenser requirements

R-numbered outcomes, intent only. `▸` marks the person's words, `[derived]` agent synthesis.

## R1 Only the dose that is due can be reached
▸ "impossible to take the wrong dose"
[derived] Between doses no pill can be reached without lifting the lid; at dose time exactly one dose is released, into the one place the parent takes pills from.

## R2 It holds a week of doses, four a day
[derived] Founder: not answered on the schedule. ▸ "complex medication" — four doses a day for seven days is the most a weekly pill organiser commonly offers (category.md), so 28 doses between refills.

## R3 A 75-year-old can refill it without tools
▸ "a refill that a 75-year-old can do"
[derived] The lid lifts off by hand, nothing needs a tool, and each dose's compartment takes a fingertip to push pills in.

## R4 The alert is loud and clear
▸ "a loud, clear alert"
[derived] Sound and light together: the sounder is rated at least 85 dB at 10 cm (the loud end of stocked self-drive buzzers), and a light on the front is lit while the dose waits.

## R5 It knows when the dose was taken
▸ "it confirms each dose was taken"
[derived] Opening the dose drawer is detected and recorded as taking the dose.

## R6 It tells the child when a dose is missed
▸ "tells me if he missed them"
[derived] It reaches the child's phone over the home's Wi-Fi; no phone or app is needed by the parent.

## R7 Nothing on it is small to operate
▸ refuses "small buttons, a touchscreen"
[derived] It has no buttons and no screen. Its only control, the drawer, is pulled with the whole fingers of one hand: the pull is wider than an older woman's hand.

## R8 The landed cost is under the target
▸ "1,000 units for a pilot. Target landed cost under $110."

## R9 The drive turns a full carousel
[derived] The motor's rated torque carries the friction of a full carousel with margin, so a dose is never left half-released.

## Shape rules
## S1 No fixing is seen from above or from the front
[derived] from formal.md (concealed fixings): every screw is put in from underneath.
## S2 One drum
[derived] from formal.md (primitive, continuous): the base and the lid are one diameter and read as one drum with one seam.
## S3 Only two things show on the outside: the drawer and one light
[derived] from principles.md P2/P3: no screen, button, lettering or vent on the faces.
## S4 One material for every printed part
[derived] from formal.md (uniform materials).
