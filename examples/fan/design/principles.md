# Principles

Traced to `discover/` (story root: it must never ask for attention).

## P1 An object, not an appliance
▸ "it looks like an object, not an appliance"
Forbids: a wire cage, a visible motor housing, a stand with a tilt knob, stickers, a model number on a face, chrome.

## P2 Nothing announces itself
[derived] from the refusal root and ▸ "blue LEDs"
Forbids: any light seen from outside, a beep, a click you can hear across the room, a status display.

## P3 Quiet beats strong
▸ "quiet above all"
Forbids: a small fast fan, a speed above the fan's quiet rating, gears that whine, a sweep that clunks at its ends.

## P4 Everything happens at the object, with one hand
▸ "one button" · refuses ▸ "a remote control, an app."
Forbids: a second control, a touch strip, a remote, any radio, a mode you must count presses to find.

## P5 Fixings live behind and below
▸ refuses "visible screws on the front"
Forbids: a screw, insert or seam line on the front face or the top; a front grille held by clips you can see.

## P6 Built by one person, from stocked parts
▸ "a solo maker" · "200 units first batch"
Forbids: custom electronics, moulds, parts only one seller makes, glue that needs a jig.

## Attributes
| Attribute | Demands | Forbids | Surfaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | the largest fan the footprint allows, run slow; soft feet | small fans, hard feet on the desk | fan, feet, sweep drive |
| Calm | slow sweep, round forms, one matte colour | fast motion, contrasting accents | head, base, motion |
| Object-like | one primitive per volume, no visible fixings | cages, knobs, visible screws | head, base, grille |
| One-handed | the button where the hand falls, on top | buttons on the back or side | base top |
| Honest | the air path is a plain opening; the button looks like a button | fake vents, hidden touch controls | grille, button |
