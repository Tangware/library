# Drum

Form vocabulary: one upright cylinder; a round grille cut into its front; the whole drum turns on a thin round foot.
Palette: one matte colour (sand/off-white PETG).
Motion character: the whole object turns, slowly, as one.
Story: [derived] a tin on the desk that breathes.
## Against the profile and the decoy's killing rules
- Describe/user/rest Must "the button reads as the only control": breaks — the button on a turning drum moves away from the hand (P4).
- Identify/buyer Must "belong with desk objects": meets.
- S4 (nothing wider than the head): meets.
- S2/round head: a drum holding a 120 mm square fan needs an inside diameter of the fan's diagonal, making the drum the largest object on the desk (S4 spirit, R8 plastic cost).
- Mechanics [derived]: the stepper would carry the whole drum's weight, not just the head.
## Readings
Naive viewers: not run (no access to people). [person] gap.
Verdict: rejected — the moving button breaks P4 and the drum diameter is set by the fan's diagonal.
