# Head and puck

Form vocabulary: a rounded-square head, the fan's own proportion, one corner radius all round; a round low puck base no wider than the head; a short round neck between them. Grille: concentric rings cut in the head's front face.
Palette: one matte colour throughout (PETG, warm off-white), the fan's black rotor seen through the rings.
Motion character: only the head turns, slowly and evenly; the puck and its button stay still.
Story: [derived] a small radio-cube that turns to look at you.
## Against the profile and the decoy's killing rules
- Describe/user/rest: the round ring grille reads as a fan; the one button on the puck's top reads as the only control — meets.
- Describe/user/in use: only the head moves — meets (sweep evenness is a firmware matter, unverified).
- Express/buyer/rest: no screw on front or top; screws under the puck and under the head — meets S1, P5.
- Exhort/user/rest: one button on the still puck — meets P4, R4.
- Identify: rounded square on a round puck reads as a desk radio/clock family — meets.
- R5, P2: no light; the board's LEDs are inside the closed puck — meets.
- S4: the puck's diameter is no wider than the head — meets by construction.
