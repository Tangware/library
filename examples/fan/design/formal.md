# Formal

| Dichotomy | Position | Attribute | Forbids |
|---|---|---|---|
| order / complexity | order | Calm | more than two volumes (head, base) plus the neck |
| reduction / richness | reduction | Object-like | decoration, texture patterns, logos on faces |
| primitive / sculpted | primitive | Object-like | freeform surfaces, lofts |
| continuous / articulated | continuous head, articulated joint | Honest | a head made of visibly separate shells on its front |
| concealed / exposed fixings | concealed | Object-like (▸ "visible screws on the front") | any screw seen from front or top |
| uniform / varied materials | uniform | Calm | a second colour or material on the shell |
| sharp / soft | soft (broken edges, small radii) | Calm | sharp corners, knife edges |
| light / heavy | heavy base, light head | Quiet (no tip, no walk) | a base narrower than the head's shadow |
| symmetric / asymmetric | symmetric about the sweep axis | Calm | an off-centre button or cable |
| matte / gloss | matte | Calm, Object-like | gloss finishes |

## Hierarchy of emphasis
Dominant: the round head and its grille. Subdominant: the round base. Subordinate: the neck, the button.

## Surface continuity
Head shell: C⁰ edges broken with a chamfer or fillet. Base: C⁰ with broken edges. Neck: C⁰.

S-rules carried into `prd.md`: S1 (concealed fixings), S2 (one head form, one corner radius), S3 (one material, one colour).
