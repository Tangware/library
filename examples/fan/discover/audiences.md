# Audiences

## Buyer
▸ "Buyer = user."
At a glance [derived]: a well-made object worth about $79 (▸ "it sells at about $79"), not a $20 appliance.

## User
▸ "remote workers at small desks."
At a glance [derived]: where the air goes, how to turn it on (▸ "one button"), and that it will not be heard on a call (▸ "quiet above all").

## Influencer
Founder: not answered. [derived] The Etsy listing photo and reviews; colleagues who see it in the corner of a video call.
At a glance [derived]: it reads as a crafted desk object on camera, not a tech gadget.

## Conflicts
### C1 Buyer reads price ("is this worth $79?"), user reads silence ("will I hear it?")
Shape serves: user ▸ "quiet above all"
Buyer served through: material, finish and packaging; the listing's story [derived]
### C2 Influencer reads on camera (a striking object), user reads a calm desk (nothing that draws the eye)
Shape serves: user ▸ "it looks like an object, not an appliance"
Influencer served through: colour and material, the Etsy photography [derived]

## Character
[derived] **The quiet craftsman** — a well-made tool that does one thing and never speaks first.
Rejected archetypes and what each forbids:
- Hero: forbids power cues (turbine looks, big blades shown off, speed numbers).
- Magician: forbids hidden tricks (a touch surface with no visible control, gesture control).
- Jester: forbids playful colours and faces.
- Ruler: forbids chrome, black-gloss premium cues.
- Caregiver: forbids soft-plush, nursery cues.
- Explorer: forbids rugged/outdoor cues.
- Rebel: forbids aggressive angles.
- Lover: forbids glossy sensual curves.
- Sage: forbids a display of information (readouts, speed numbers).
- Innocent: forbids pastel toy cues.
- Everyman: forbids the plain white plastic appliance look (it is the residual of the category).
- Creator (kept as the nearest neighbour): forbids nothing extra.
