## Why this, why you
▸ "a solo maker who sells desk accessories on Etsy and wants a first electronic product."

## Why a physical product and not an app
[derived] It refuses an app; everything it does is done at the object, with one button.

## Why this user, why now
▸ "remote workers at small desks."
[derived] Their desk is also their meeting room: the fan is on camera and on the microphone.

## What has to be true
▸ "Target landed cost under $35; it sells at about $79."
[derived] Every part is stocked and a solo maker can assemble it with a soldering iron and a hex key.

## Purpose and refusals
Refusals: visible front screws · blue LEDs · a remote control · an app · [derived] noise.
Root [derived]: **it must never ask for attention.** Front screws and blue LEDs draw the eye; a remote and an app draw the hand and the phone; noise draws the ear. The root decides new cases: a beep on button press is refused; a status light, if any, is warm and off by default.

## Story structure
[derived] The product does not guide the user (one button, nothing to learn), so the brand may play guide: the shop page tells the "quiet object" story; the object itself only stays quiet.
