Strandbeest: Theo Jansen's walking linkage as a real machine. Twelve printed legs on one six-throw crank, driven by one gearmotor from a 4-AA holder, under six printed sails. The sails are for show: the gearmotor walks it.

Build it in Tangware Studio, which runs the checks.

Link proportions: Theo Jansen's, as tabled at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jansen%27s_linkage (the table cites Nansai et al. and Giesbrecht). Motor, battery holder and switch: Pololu 3069, MPD BC4AAW and E-Switch RD11131121; their maker CAD is downloaded on the first build. The rocker switch sits in a wall on the deck, at the battery holder's motor side. The cells are Energizer EN91 AA, modelled at the size DigiKey lists for them, since no maker CAD exists for them.
