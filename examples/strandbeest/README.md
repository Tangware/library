Strandbeest: Theo Jansen's walking linkage as a real machine. Twelve printed legs on one six-throw crank, driven by one gearmotor from a 4-AA holder, under six printed sails. The sails are for show: the gearmotor walks it.

Build it in Tangware Studio, which runs the checks.

Link proportions: Theo Jansen's, as tabled at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jansen%27s_linkage (the table cites Nansai et al. and Giesbrecht). Motor, battery holder and switch: Pololu 3069, MPD BC4AAW and E-Switch RD11131121; their maker CAD is downloaded on the first build. The rocker switch sits in a wall on the deck, at the battery holder's motor side. The cells are Energizer E91 AA, modelled at their datasheet's largest size, since no maker CAD exists for them.
