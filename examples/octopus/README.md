Octopus: a desk robot with eight arms. Each arm is one gearmotor, a crank and a drive rod swinging four printed segments, whose crossed rods curl the arm as it swings. Four AA cells under the mantle run all eight motors; each motor free-runs, so the arms wave on their own.

Build it in Tangware Studio, which runs the checks.

Proportions are read by eye from the robotic octopus clip in https://github.com/elvarofficial/teardown (`docs/octopus.gif`); no source ships for that machine. Motor, battery holder and switch: Pololu 3069, MPD BC4AAW and E-Switch RD11131121; their maker CAD is downloaded on the first build. The rocker switch sits in a recess in the mantle, just above its flange. The cells are Energizer E91 AA, modelled at their datasheet's largest size, since no maker CAD exists for them.
